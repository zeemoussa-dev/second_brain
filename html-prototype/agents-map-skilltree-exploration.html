HOW TO READ THIS MAP

6 sections · 14 agents mapped · 1 Knowledge Base
THE RULE

Every agent belongs to exactly one Section (a domain — Capture, People, Knowledge Q&A today) and exactly one Type, which sets its ring: Worker (outermost — runs a recurring capture job), Expert (middle — answers questions over the vault), or Producer (innermost — maintains a living document). The Knowledge Base sits at the center of every Section's wheel; agents read from it and Producers write back to it.

HUBS

Each Section has one Hub. Cross-Section requests always route Hub → Hub, never agent-to-agent directly — keyword-matched to the Hub best suited to answer, per each agent's own configured keywords.

READING A NODE

Click any agent to open its detail card — settings, actions, a live chat, and its communication history. Click a Section's Hub to focus that Section full-size; the chevrons at the screen edges page directly to the next Section without returning to the overview first.
